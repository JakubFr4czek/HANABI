# Oni asset shortlist (research only)

Status: **nothing downloaded**, nothing added to `assets/`, `CREDITS.md` or `LICENSES.md`. Licences, polycounts and download counts below were read from the listing pages on 2026-10-28 (Sketchfab pages show the licence and triangle count; OpenGameArt pages often don't show a polycount, marked "not stated").

The current boss is the procedural project-CC0 Oni (`oni_mask_run.glb`, `oni_body_run.glb`, `oni_kanabo_run.glb`, generated by `tools/gen_oni_*_glb.py`).

## Finding

No free CC0/CC-BY **full-body oni** at premium quality turned up. The only oni-specific free models are **masks**. The full-body options are generic demons that would need re-texturing and a horn/kanabo pass to read as an oni. Practical use of this list: replace or enhance the **mask** (the most visible part in VR), and keep the procedural body and kanabō. All Quest budgets (≤345 draw calls, 72 fps) apply, so the heavier meshes below would need decimating.

### Notes per model

1. **thanoskt mask**: the cheapest sensible mask. A Zenodo record also lists it as CC BY 4.0 (https://zenodo.org/records/10237232). Needs a Quest-friendly material and a normal map from the existing project pipeline. Attribution required.
2. **Abderrahim_Brs mask**: same budget as #1, stylised; compare the two visually before choosing. Attribution required.
3. **dammne mask**: the most detailed and most "oni" sculpt, but 230k tris is far over budget. Only usable as a **bake source** (normal map onto a ~2k-tri mesh), which is a derivative work, so CC BY attribution still applies.
4. **campos demon**: the only full-body option with a sensible polycount (16.6k is high for a Quest boss and should be decimated to ~8k). Not an oni by design, so it needs horns and a kanabō. Rig not stated; check on the page before relying on it.
5. **nubux Night Demon**: very light and already rigged, but the 1520-face silhouette is plain and the Rigify rig is described by the author as "might not be perfect". The ZIP/RAR is an unusual container; check the model format before committing.

### Considered and rejected

- **Mophs "Demon" and "Chained Demon"** (OpenGameArt, CC-BY 3.0 plus GPL 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0, basic rig, polycount not stated): the licences work, but the pages describe a basic, non-IK rig, no stated polycount, and they are not oni designs (not visually reviewed).
- **Ao Oni** by spiffatron (Sketchfab, CC BY, ~800k tris): far too heavy for Quest.
- **nubux Ogre lowpoly** (OpenGameArt, CC-BY 3.0): not rigged, ogre rather than oni.
- **obliviist Cyber Demon asset pack** (itch.io, CC0, rigged, FBX, polycount not stated): the only CC0 option found, but the PSX/cyberpunk look doesn't fit.
- **"Kanabo Of The Oni"** by CGnewbie (19.2k tris): sold on the Sketchfab Store, so not free.
- **Oni Half Mask** on Thangs: CC BY-NC-ND, which forbids adaptation and commercial use.
- Several Oni models on CGTrader and RenderHub are paid.

## Recommendation

Use #1 or #2 as the mask only if it clearly beats the procedural mask in a headset screenshot comparison. If it does, bake normal detail from #3 onto it, keep the procedural body and kanabō, and add a CC BY 4.0 line to `CREDITS.md` and `LICENSES.md` (author, title, link, "modified: retextured/decimated"). If it doesn't, keep the current boss. Do not adopt #4 or #5 as the body without an art-direction pass.

Before any download, re-check the licence on the model page (Sketchfab licences can change) and keep the download in an empty directory outside `assets/` until it is converted and size-checked (≤50 MB repo limit).
