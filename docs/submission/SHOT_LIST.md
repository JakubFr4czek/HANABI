## Rules for the cut
- No camera roll, no fast head turns in FP (smoothing hides small ones only).
- Never show the debug/perf HUD (F9) or desktop overlays.
- Captions in English, short, lower third, white on transparent; at most 5 in the whole video.
- Music: the in-game ambience and impacts only (Vacuous BGM must not be used).
